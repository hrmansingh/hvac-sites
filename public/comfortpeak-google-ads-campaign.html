03 · Keyword strategy

Use tightly controlled intent at launch. Search-term data then determines what deserves more budget, a negative, or a new ad group.

High-intent themes

Control principle

Match type is only one layer of control. Geography, negatives, search-term review, ad relevance and landing-page alignment all matter.

Negative keyword framework

Not “set and forget”

Negative lists should evolve from actual search-term data. Useful new queries should be retained rather than blindly excluded.

NorthDemand principle: a campaign that produces cheap leads but poor jobs is not performing well. Optimization should increasingly reflect lead quality and client economics.

07 · Optimization system

Once data accumulates, improve the system in a defined order rather than changing everything at once.

Search-term quality

Remove irrelevant queries, promote valuable themes, and expand negative coverage based on evidence.

Ad + landing alignment

Test messaging around the actual problem, service clarity, local relevance and call intent.

Lead quality

Compare conversions with qualified calls, booked jobs and ultimately the economics of the customer.
