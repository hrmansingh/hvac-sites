Who We Are

In 1988, Ken McGuire started McGuire & Company to distribute temperature controls and HVAC specialties to mechanical contractors seeking knowledgeable, friendly advice and the right parts for varied applications.

As systems became more complex, our services expanded to include complete, turn-key temperature control and computer based monitoring systems. Today, hundreds of buildings throughout Maine, New Hampshire and Massachusetts operate with our controls.

What We Do

McGuire Controls sells, installs and services temperature, lighting and access control systems in commercial, industrial and residential buildings.
