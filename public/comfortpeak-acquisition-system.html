04 · Measurement stack

Measure the chain.

Each metric answers a different question. None should be treated as the entire story.

TrafficSearch quality

Search terms, CTR, wasted queries and ad relevance.

ConversionLead rate

Calls, forms and landing-page conversion behavior.

QualityQualified leads

Relevant opportunities after basic screening.

BusinessBooked jobs

Appointments and downstream outcomes where available.

Important: A cheaper lead is not automatically a better lead. The useful comparison is cost against the quality and business value of the resulting opportunities.
05 · Optimization engine

The feedback loop.

The campaign gets better by using real lead outcomes to make better decisions about traffic, messaging and conversion.

COLLECT

What came in?

Search terms, calls, forms, lead quality and job outcomes.

→
DIAGNOSE

Where is the waste?

Irrelevant traffic, weak intent, friction or poor follow-up.

→
IMPROVE

What changes?

Keywords, negatives, ads, targeting, page and process.

Then repeat. Optimization is a continuous operating process, not a one-time campaign setup.
06 · Example decision

Show the thinking, not just the dashboard.

Example scenario using no invented performance numbers.

Signal

Calls are coming in, but some are outside the service area.

  • Review the search terms producing those calls.
  • Check location-targeting configuration.
  • Identify whether the same pattern repeats.
Response

Tighten the source of irrelevant demand.

  • Add appropriate negative keywords where justified.
  • Refine geographic targeting where appropriate.
  • Measure whether qualified-call share improves.
